How to read this

A sawtooth is the product working: decay, then an extension, then decay again. A straight decline is the control: guinea-pig B was calibrated once on 5 September and left alone, so its two observations agree on one expiry — ledger 4,793,687, about 21 September 2026. A's steps each link to the transaction that made them; the second step's timing was arranged by raising the threshold, the response was not. The shared code entry binds all three contracts and expires about 20 October 2026 at ledger 5,290,829.
