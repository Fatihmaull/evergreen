Alerts

Optional emails can be turned off. Required ones cannot. Signing in with email cannot move XLM.

To

alerts-user@example.com

undetermined

A sign-in code was accepted for this address once. Whether a later alert arrives is undetermined.

Sending

undetermined

Whether any alert email is being sent is undetermined. No mail in the inbox is not health.

Optional

On unless you turn one off. A missing saved choice means on.

  • Allowance about to expire

    The allowance autopilot uses is about to expire. How far ahead is undetermined.

    on

  • Approaching expiry

    Email when an entry you watch is approaching expiry and autopilot is off for it. The ledger count is undetermined. Any day count is an estimate; the ledger is the truth.

    One choice for every entry you watch. There is no per-contract switch.

    on

  • Extend succeeded

    Autopilot extended this entry. The email is a receipt of that extend. It is not a payment, and this page does not move XLM.

    on

Required

These alerts are required for autopilot users and cannot be turned off. The mark is the email's severity, not a contract's health.

  • Pre-flight failed

    warning

    The pre-flight check failed, so nothing was pulled from your wallet and the entry was not extended.

  • Allowance missing or used up

    warning

    Your allowance is missing, expired, or its 30-day budget is used up. Autopilot cannot extend this entry until you approve a new allowance.

  • Extend failed, refund under way

    critical

    The extend failed after the pull. The whole pull, fee included, is being refunded. The entry is still not protected.

  • Refund delayed

    critical

    A refund owed to you is delayed. It is still owed and will be sent to your wallet.

  • Unexplained charge

    critical

    Reconciliation found a difference between what Evergreen recorded and what the chain shows for your wallet.

  • Testnet reset

    warning

    The Stellar testnet appears to have been reset. Protection state for this entry starts over.

  • Restore lost changes

    warning

    Evergreen data was restored from a backup. Recent changes for this entry may be missing; which ones is undetermined.

At most 5 optional emails are sent in 24 hours. Required emails are not counted in that 5.

No alert email is not health. It can mean nothing happened, or that sending did not.
