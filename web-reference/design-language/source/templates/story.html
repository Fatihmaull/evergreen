Absence is not health.

A clean scan covers only what was asked for. Each answer carries a shape as well as a colour.

healthy

Above 120,960 ledgers, about seven days.

warning

Inside 120,960 ledgers, about seven days.

critical

At or below 17,280 ledgers, about one day.

archived

Past its final live ledger. A temporary entry is deleted instead.

undetermined

The check ran and cannot conclude from what it was given.

incomplete

The network refused or failed. The scan did not complete.
